Get to know Magic Marta

31 Jan 2021 · Hamish Urquhart

Quickfire questions with Magic Marta

How did you get into magic and kid’s entertainment?

My partner is a professional entertainer and magician. So, through the fun and magic he showed me, I formed a love for magic I never knew I had.

He taught me it’s not about the props and tricks, it’s all about the fun and the interaction with children. That’s the real magic!

Do you remember your first show?

I’ll never forget it. I was so nervous, not because I didn’t know the tricks, but because I wasn’t sure how the kids would react to me.

I remember walking through the doors of this big house in Malahide and there must have been around 50 kids, and I thought to myself ‘Well there’s only me and them, let’s do this. All I can do is my best and be myself’.

And to my great surprise they warmed to me immediately and the show was a big success.

Looking back, I don’t know why I was so nervous. But I feel it’s important to always be a little bit nervous because that energy encourages you to be the best you can be, and children of all ages inherently sense that.

Tell us a bit about your regular show

My Magic show is pretty much tailored to suit different customers’ needs.

It is full of magic, music, games, treats, balloons, puppetry, face painting, and includes a super cool magical gift!

With my entertainment skills and their imaginations, together we create an unforgettable hour of laughter and fun!

That’s a lot of different activities – how did you learn all of those things?

I was always good at art, I enjoy learning new skills, and I am self-taught.

I used to practice face painting on my niece and nephew. I needed models so badly that I went to the extreme and I made my own model… my daughter Charlee.

She loves it; at least four times a week she’s asks me “Mummy let’s play funny faces!”

Have you had any memorable shows that perhaps didn’t go to plan?

Yes, there was one I will never forget!… It was the month of May, Communion season, probably the busiest month for all kids’ entertainers.

I arrived at the hotel, set up, and needed to use the bathroom. Then my biggest nightmare happened – the lock in the bathroom door broke and I got locked in! I was stuck there for a good half an hour.

The poor manager of the hotel had to drive to B&Q to get tools and then to rescue me.

I got the biggest cheer when I got out and of course a slagging that I was ‘some magician’…they all said ‘why couldn’t you use your magic to get out?’ ‘I’m a magician not an escapologist!’ I replied.

And how did you build the business?

I could tell you I spent a lot of money on face book ads, websites, advertising and the like, but actually it was word of mouth.

I built my own website, I got stuck in, and I got out there and just started performing.

The business grew and grew from parents and customers recommending me.

You see, when you do something you love, it’s not work – it’s a joy and people pick up on that and they want to share it with their friends.

Who are your biggest inspirations?

My Mam & Dad inspired me a lot and gave me the confidence and the skills in life to work hard, to be honest and to be kind to people. My partner Dave taught me how to be funny, engaging and entertaining.

When I was only starting out, I was worried; what if the trick didn’t go right?

Dave explained to me that only you know if the trick went right or wrong, the real magic is the fun.

This advice has stood me well. If only he was as optimistic about my cooking! Or ‘burning’ as he calls it!!

Why do you enjoy doing what you do?

I can’t think of a better way to make a living; bringing fun and joy and a little magic to people’s lives…. can you?

In what other profession can you see the charm and unreserved happiness in people. Children are the real magic in life!

What makes a great kids’ entertainer?

I don’t think you could make someone a kids’ entertainer, I think it’s either in them or it’s not.

If money is your motivation, well then you will never be an entertainer because it’s not a life with a guaranteed steady income; there’s no sick or holiday pay and you never know from one week to the next if you’re working.

But when you enjoy what it is you do, the work comes! I think you unconsciously attract it with your energy that in turn attracts people towards you.

You prosper from that, I’m sure there’s a little LOOK involved too. That’s not a typo or my Polish English (!); it’s my philosophy ‘Look and you will find!’

I gather you have recently had a second baby – that was good timing?!

Yes, my second daughter, Nicci, was born in the middle of this pandemic; it was great timing to be on maternity leave and to really enjoy motherhood.

All my shows were postponed so it gave me priceless time to spend with my girls, and my biggest kid Dave Young! lol

How are you planning to adapt your business in light of the current restrictions?

Well, the only option at the moment seems to be virtual.

It would not be my first preference because I think you can’t beat the interaction with a live audience. We built a studio where myself and my partner perform from.

I’ve performed some virtual shows over the Christmas, and they have gone exceptionally well, but I really can’t wait to get back to doing what I love to do, which is live shows.

There’s no comparison to the atmosphere of a live show versus a computer screen.

You know the old saying “the smell of the crowd, the roar of the greasepaint!”

Did I say that right? Hey I’m Polrish! (That’s what you get when you mix Polish with Irish…Polrish!)

Tell us something we wouldn’t know about you

My first love in life is my partner and my two girls, my second love is entertaining, and my third love is cooking.

In a way it’s entertainment for me to take something from nothing and turn it into something beautiful and then eat it.

Now that’s magic! haha

What do you think of PartyWizz?

What I like about PartyWizz is they are one of the few entertainment companies that take a genuine interest in their performers.

They are very easy to work with and their ability to present their clients with an entertainer suitable for their needs is insightful.

Thank you PartyWizz, it was a pleasure talking to you, and I’m looking forward to a prosperous 2021.

No matter what happens, I will keep the little ones entertained and keep the magic going! After all there are no restrictions on imagination!

St. Patrick's Day Fun Ideas

24 Feb 2017 · Magic Marta

Kids parties have become hugely popular over the St. Partick's Day Bank Holiday weekend. There's no better time to let your little ones invite over a few friends for some holiday fun. Here's a few fun ideas for St. Patrick's Day including party crafts, games and food...

1. Silly Mcgilly

Start a new St. Patrick's Day tradition by inviting the magic of Silly McGilly into your home or classroom. Silly McGilly is an adorable leprechaun who loves playing fun little tricks on children throughout the St. Patrick's Day season. Read Silly McGilly's book with your children and place his doll in the window overnight.

If you are lucky, Silly might just play a trick on you as you sleep!

2. Cool party games

Gold Hunt

Hide gold coins around the house or garden and have the kids hunt for them. The child that collects the most coins wins!

Kiss the Blarney Stone

Print out photo of the Blarney Stone and hang it on a wall. Put lipstick or face paint on the little ones, blindfold them and send them to plant a kiss on the Blarney Stone. Players who come closest to kissing the stone win a prize.

St. Patrick's Day Parade

Can't make it to the parade? No problem :)

Gather fun props, wigs, flags & banners and let them parade on their own, right in your home and garden!

Irish dancing competition

Just play some Irish music and let the kids show off their best attempts at Irish dancing.

3. Fun green & rainbow food

Practically any food can be dyed green with a little food coloring!

How about green pasta with pesto for a quick snack and green cupcakes and some rainbow jelly for a nice dessert. You could make your own shamrocks milkshakes - that's always a hit!

8 Fun Ideas for Kids at Weddings

17 Sep 2015 · Magic Marta

The discussion of whether to invite children to a wedding always becomes a passionate one. In one corner, you have people who think kids add a certain magic to the atmosphere. In the other corner, you have those who feel as though that "magic" is more the dark variety - the screaming, the messing, the ruining. But including kids in your festivities doesn't have to be a horror movie in the making. Follow these guidelines to ensure that your wedding is fun for all ages...

1. Kids Only Space

Give them their own space so that they feel all grown up. These cute signs will make them feel important and you’ll know exactly where to find them.

2. Cute Place Names

Have some fun with the table setting and use letter shaped cookies to spell out the littlest members of the wedding party’s names. Other cute alternatives are tags tied to lollipops or little Lego men figurines next to their name card!

3. A Special Toast

Let little folk be a part of the toast with their own special kid-friendly drink. Old time favourites like milk and cookies or milk topped with mini doughnuts will go down a treat as will milkshakes, smoothies, mini bottles of lemonade or hot chocolates for chillier winter wedding celebrations.

4. Craft Corner

Swap your fancy table cloth for a sheet of Kraft paper or some blackboard place mats and leave a bunch of colouring pencils, chalk or crayons in the middle so they can doodle as they dine. Bonus – you don’t have to worry about spillages, stains and dry cleaning bills.

5. Party Bags

Give them their own little stash of goodies to keep them amused. Fill party bags with bits and bobs from sweets and games to colouring pencils, jigsaws, Lego, bubbles and stickers to pass the hours.

6. Fun & Games

Games are a great way to keep kids occupied. If you’re having a summer wedding, set up some outdoor games to let them burn some energy. Or if you’re worried about the weather, some board games will work a treat indoors. There are also some great printables available online that you can download for free (Martha Stewart have an I Spy game). We also love the idea of having a big bowl of Lego as a centrepiece so they can dig and get creative after dinner.

7. Movie Night

One of the handiest ways to keep them happy is with a favourite movie! Check with your venue whether there’s a room close by that you can use for a bit of a movie night or if you’ve no one to keep an eye on them, create a movie corner close to where you’re sitting. Pop on a Disney fave and let them wile away the hours with some popcorn and a few sweets.

8. The Professionals

An easy, stress free way to entertain the kids is by hiring a professional! If you’re after an activity to keep them busy, there are a wealth of amazing magicians you can hire for a few hours to kill some time and keep them laughing like Marta’s Magic Show.
